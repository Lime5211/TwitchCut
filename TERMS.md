# TwitchCut — Terms of Service

TwitchCut is a personal, non-commercial desktop tool. It runs only on its owner's computer and is used by its owner only.

1. TwitchCut cuts the owner's selected Twitch stream recordings into short vertical clips.
2. When the owner presses "Send to TikTok", TwitchCut uploads the selected clip to the owner's own TikTok account as a draft (TikTok Content Posting API, video.upload). TwitchCut never publishes anything by itself: the owner reviews, edits and publishes the draft in the TikTok app.
3. TwitchCut is provided "as is", without any warranty.
4. The owner is responsible for the content they publish and for following the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
