# TwitchCut — Privacy Policy

TwitchCut is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner on their own computer.

- **What data is used:** TikTok access tokens and basic account info (open_id, display name, avatar) of the owner's own TikTok accounts, received after the owner logs in through TikTok Login Kit.
- **Why:** only to upload the owner's own clips to their own TikTok accounts as drafts (scope video.upload).
- **Where it is stored:** only locally on the owner's computer (folder `data`). Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API.
- **Sharing:** data is never sold or shared with third parties.
- **Deletion:** the owner can disconnect any account in TwitchCut ("Status" page), which deletes its tokens, and can revoke access in the TikTok app settings at any time.
